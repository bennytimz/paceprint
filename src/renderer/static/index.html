Before we start

Find the breathing pace that suits you.

Most breathing apps assume six breaths a minute suits everyone. Paceprint measures which slow pace your heart rhythm and breathing move together best, from your webcam, and refuses to show numbers it can't trust.

Let's get a clean signal

    Tips for a good reading
    • Even light from the front. No window or lamp behind you.
    • Avoid flickering light: some screens and bulbs flicker.
    • Laptop on a table, not on your lap. Sit back so your chest and waist are in view.
    • Plain, lighter clothing reads best. Dark or tight stripes can confuse it.
    • Stay still and don't talk while measuring.
